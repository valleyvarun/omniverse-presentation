Projects involve many people working across different tools, file types, and locations, which makes coordination slow and fragmented. With NVIDIA’s cloud services integrated into the Omniverse Launcher, teams can collaborate in a shared digital space where project files, updates, and workflows stay continuously in sync. 
Multiple users can work on the same project simultaneously, view real-time changes, track progress, and manage versions without the confusion of scattered files or outdated information. Cloud-based coordination ensures that everyone—designers, engineers, creators, and AI agents—has access to the same up-to-date context, making teamwork faster, clearer, and far more efficient.
