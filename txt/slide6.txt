A cohesive aesthetic helps create a shared identity, making developers and end-users feel part of one creative platform rather than a collection of isolated tools. 
A redesigned launcher can communicate this unity clearly, helping people understand Omniverse—which often feels abstract even though it forms the very concrete foundation for the tools that shape our physical and digital worlds. 
Developers could also add their software or extensions to an Omniverse app store, making it easy for the community to discover, access, and try new tools. This brings users and developers together into a connected, collaborative ecosystem where ideas, feedback, and innovation flow more openly.
