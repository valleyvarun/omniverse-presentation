Multimodal AI models can be trained using the millions of 3D and other digital assets stored on cloud storage, learning from real project data. 

A community asset store would allow creators to share the models they build across different software tools. The AI agent could also reuse existing assets—placing them directly into a scene inside any software—rather than generating everything from scratch. 

Simulations further strengthen the AI’s abilities: <span style="color: #aaa !important; font-style: italic;">for example, it can “study” a 3D model by moving a virtual camera around it, learning how images correspond to 3D geometry and improving both its image generation and image-to-3D reconstruction skills. </span>The AI could train itself by attempting to recreate these shared assets through operating the software, continuously improving its creative and technical capabilities.
